    How to play

    Goal

    Four races colonize the moon from one shared Landing Base in the middle. Build the base with the most points when nobody can build any more.

    Your turn

    1. Build every piece on your Landing pad. A piece that doesn't fit yet can go into Storage (build a Small Storage first) — otherwise you have to scrap it.
    2. Draw capsules — a random amount each turn (Humans get +1).
    3. Order new pieces in the market. They show up under Requests and land on your pad at the start of your next turn.

    Puzzle pieces

    Every side of a piece is a wall, an exit (a docking port that sticks out) or an entrance (a docking hatch). An exit against an entrance opens a passage you can walk through. Two exits can never touch, an exit can't push into a wall, and an exit can't point off the moon. While placing, a green ✓ or red ✕ shows on every side that touches something.

    Hallways must connect to a hallway (any player's). All your other buildings may only connect to your own hallways.

    Power

    Solar Plants and Anti-Matter Labs make power; most other buildings use it. Resources, rooms and storage get power first, then facilities — highest points first. A facility without power scores nothing.

    Scoring

    Only facilities score: base points × (1 + powered guest rooms within 4 hallways). Guest rooms may belong to anyone. Your own race's facilities also need one of your powered race resources. Example: a Star Meal (5) with 3 guest rooms nearby scores 5 × 4 = 20.

    The yellow badge above a facility shows what it scores right now. Hover a building to see the calculation and green lines to the guest rooms that count; hover a player in Races for their full list.

    Races

    • Lustroms — Inner Glow: +30 power at all times.
    • Humans — Research Grants: +1 capsule every turn.
    • Machines — Compression: every Small Storage holds 2 pieces, plus +15 power.
    • boZob — Slime Battery: +10 power at all times.

    Game end

    The game ends when three rounds pass without anyone building, when the moon is full, or when all pieces are used. Click a player on the end screen to see where every point came from.

    Controls

    • Scroll zoom · drag move · double-click reset view
    • R, arrows, WASD or right-click rotate the piece
    • Q / E or middle-drag turn the camera (3D) · 2D / 3D button switches view
    • ← Lobby leaves the game; you can jump back in from the lobby.

    Other players' requests are shown face down in Races: you see the puzzle shape, not the building. On the website without a game server you can play against computers or watch them; online play against people needs the game server.
