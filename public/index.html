    How to play
    1. Build every piece that landed on your landing pad. A Small Storage lets you keep one for later.
    2. Draw capsules — a random amount each turn.
    3. Order new pieces with your capsules. They land at the start of your next turn.

    Pieces are puzzle pieces. Each side is a wall, an entrance (notch) or an exit (tab). Exit against entrance makes a passage. Two exits can never touch, and an exit can't push into a wall.

    Hallways must connect to a hallway (any colour). Your buildings may only connect to your own hallways.

    Buildings need power: Solar Plants and Anti-Matter Labs supply it. Resources and rooms get power first, facilities after.

    Scoring: every powered facility scores its points × the number of Rooms within 4 hallways. Race facilities also need one of your powered race resources.
